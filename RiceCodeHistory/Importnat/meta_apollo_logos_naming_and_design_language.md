# Meta Apollo Logos — Naming System & Design Language Notes

> Working naming/design-language document as of 2026-09-17.
>
> This is intentionally a living document. Names may evolve as the ecosystem grows.

---

# 1. Current Umbrella Name

The current official working umbrella name is:

# **Meta Apollo Logos**

This is the name currently being used for the larger project ecosystem.

Possible repo/directory spellings are **not final**.

Candidates may include:

```text
meta-apollo-logos
metapollogos
metaapollologos
```

Do not lock the slug until it has been tested in actual daily use.

---

# 2. Why the Name Exists

The original aesthetic was often described simply as:

```text
Neon CRT
```

That was useful as a surface-level description, but it did not describe the deeper design philosophy.

The project is trying to express something broader:

- retro technology
- records
- cassette tapes
- VHS
- CRT displays
- glitch effects
- pixel art
- scanlines
- clicks and mechanical sounds
- physical-looking buttons
- overly complex displays where a simple graphic would technically work
- neon and high-energy color
- maximal decoration
- tactile interfaces
- nostalgia
- futuristic technology
- cyberpunk / dystopian atmosphere
- mid-century and postwar American visual influence
- playful Gen-Z maximalism
- "dopamine maxing"
- warmth and humanity inside technological decay

The feeling is not simply:

> old technology

and not simply:

> futuristic technology

It is closer to:

> **a flashy, playful new layer built over an aging technological world.**

---

# 3. The Emotional Core

A major idea that emerged during naming discussion:

> The world may feel like it is burning, but people still look backward, recover things that felt human, tactile, joyful, and meaningful, and build something playful from them.

The nostalgia is not only nostalgia for nostalgia's sake.

It can function as:

- resistance to sterility
- resistance to disposable design
- recovery of tactility
- recovery of personality
- recovery of play
- recovery of human scale
- raw material for imagining another future

The design is allowed to be excessive.

Empty space can be treated as an opportunity for:

- information
- ornament
- motion
- texture
- controls
- color
- personality

This is part of the "dopamine maxing" side of the aesthetic.

# 4. Important Working Concepts

Several phrases emerged that should remain available as subcategories, design-language labels, project names, repo names, themes, or future branches.

## Post-Apollo

This became especially important.

It has multiple meanings.

### Apollo as sun god

"Post-Apollo" can mean:

> after the sun has gone

This gives the phrase a poetic, warm, almost spiritual feeling.

It suggests:

- afterlight
- sunset
- warmth remembered after darkness
- carrying light forward after the source is gone

### Apollo as technological optimism

Apollo also evokes:

- the Space Age
- American technological optimism
- NASA-era futurism
- the promise that technology would make life brighter
- mid-century visions of the future

So "Post-Apollo" can also mean:

> after the bright technological future people were promised failed to arrive exactly as imagined

That overlap is one of the strongest ideas in the naming system.

---

# 5. Hyperanalog

Another strong working phrase:

# **Hyperanalog**

This describes the tactile/maximal side of the design.

Possible associations:

- knobs
- meters
- switches
- CRTs
- tape
- vinyl
- cassettes
- dials
- physical media
- mechanical interaction
- visible machinery
- interfaces that expose process instead of hiding it

The "hyper" side allows this to become exaggerated rather than historically accurate.

It is not historical reenactment.

It is:

> analog language turned up beyond reality.

---

# 6. CRT Pop

Another useful sub-label:

# **CRT Pop**

This describes the brighter, more immediately visual side.

It can cover:

- bright color
- neon
- scanlines
- glowing text
- pixel graphics
- retro displays
- playful HUDs
- intense interface decoration
- high-energy visual feedback

This can be useful for individual themes or repositories without needing to carry the full philosophical weight of Meta Apollo Logos.

---

# 7. Afterglow / Afterlight

"Afterglow" and "Afterlight" were explored as poetic concepts.

They can still be useful names for:

- themes
- color schemes
- shader packs
- visual modes
- wallpaper collections
- project releases
- animation presets

They represent:

> the light that remains after the bright thing itself has passed.

They fit naturally under the broader Post-Apollo idea.

---

# 8. Revival

"Post-Apollo Revival" was discussed as a near-complete phrase.

"Revival" has several useful resonances:

- cultural revival
- technological revival
- spiritual revival
- religious/Christian resonance
- rediscovery of old forms
- bringing forgotten things back into use

Even if it does not become the umbrella name, it remains a useful word in the project's vocabulary.

---

# 9. Christian / Spiritual Layer

The naming discussion also explored adding a warmer, more human, potentially Christian dimension.

The goal is not necessarily to make every project name explicitly religious.

Instead, words can carry layered resonance.

Examples discussed:

- revival
- logos
- light
- soul
- afterlight
- Apollo / sun imagery

"Logos" is especially useful because it can carry:

- word
- reason
- principle
- order
- philosophical Greek roots
- Christian theological resonance

This gives Meta Apollo Logos more human depth than a purely technological name.

---

# 10. Why "Meta Apollo Logos"

The name grew from an attempt to combine:

```text
Post-Apollo
+
Hyperanalog
+
Greek-root wordplay
+
human/spiritual meaning
```

It is intentionally somewhat strange and playful.

That is a feature, not necessarily a bug.

The project itself is maximal, layered, and willing to create new combinations from older materials.

The name can behave the same way.

---

# 11. Pronunciation / Flexibility

The exact spoken rhythm is allowed to evolve.

Part of the appeal is that it is fun to say even when it is said imperfectly.

The name should not become so rigid that the project loses its playful character.

Possible shorthand or derived forms may emerge naturally.

One already discussed:

# **Metapollo**

This works well as a shorter modifier for component projects.

Example:

```text
SwayFX Metapollo
```

Possible intended reading:

> SwayFX after Apollo / after the sun / after the bright technological promise.

That layered meaning is desirable.

---

# 12. Naming Philosophy for the Ecosystem

The ecosystem should have a **family resemblance**, not a rigid naming prefix.

The Apple comparison was useful:

- some products share a strong naming pattern
- others do not
- they still feel like they belong in the same ecosystem

Likewise, Meta Apollo Logos projects do not all need to begin with:

```text
Meta
Apollo
Metapollo
```

Instead, names should feel related through:

- shared vocabulary
- shared mood
- recognizable role
- recognizable upstream technology
- recurring imagery
- recurring philosophical concepts

---

# 13. Names Should Still Tell You What the Project Is

Purely poetic names can become hard to navigate.

A strong naming rule:

> A project name should usually tell you either what it is built on or what role it performs.

Examples:

```text
SwayFX Metapollo
Kitty <creative modifier>
Fastfetch <creative modifier>
Quickshell <creative modifier>
```

This keeps repositories discoverable.

The creative portion can carry the ecosystem identity.

---

# 14. Display Name vs Repository Slug

The human-facing name and GitHub slug do not have to be identical.

Example:

```text
Display name:
SwayFX Metapollo

Repo slug:
swayfx-metapollo
```

This is useful because:

- the display name can be expressive
- the slug can stay predictable
- users can still identify the underlying technology immediately

# 16. Possible Component Naming Pattern

Future component projects may use:

```text
<Upstream/Role> <World/Style Name>
```

Examples purely as pattern demonstrations:

```text
SwayFX Metapollo
Kitty Afterlight
Fastfetch CRT Pop
Quickshell Revival
```

These exact names are not decisions.

The important part is the structure:

```text
recognizable component
+
creative family name
```

---

# 17. Avoid Naming Everything the Same Way

Do not force:

```text
Metapollo Kitty
Metapollo Fastfetch
Metapollo Sway
Metapollo Quickshell
Metapollo Neovim
```

unless it genuinely feels good.

That would become mechanical and lose the family-resemblance idea.

Some projects can use:

```text
Metapollo
Afterlight
Post-Apollo
Hyperanalog
CRT Pop
Revival
```

or future related words.

The ecosystem should feel connected without looking templated.

# 18. Design Language Hierarchy

A useful current hierarchy:

```text
Meta Apollo Logos
        │
        ├── umbrella ecosystem / worldview
        │
        ├── Post-Apollo
        │      poetic / historical / dystopian-future layer
        │
        ├── Hyperanalog
        │      tactile / mechanical / maximal interface layer
        │
        ├── CRT Pop
        │      bright / playful / neon / pixel layer
        │
        ├── Afterglow / Afterlight
        │      poetic visual/theme language
        │
        └── Revival
               cultural / spiritual / rediscovery layer
```

These categories can overlap.

They are not strict genres.

# 19. The American Layer

The project should have an identifiable American quality without reducing itself to flags or literal patriotic imagery.

Potential influences include:

- mid-century American futurism
- roadside neon
- diners
- jukeboxes
- radios
- suburban electronics
- NASA / Apollo-era optimism
- domestic technology
- early home computing
- broadcast television
- mall / arcade / pop-culture excess
- old consumer electronics
- Gen-Z reinterpretation of 20th-century American objects

The American influence should feel embedded in the world, not pasted on top.

---

# 20. The Dystopian Layer

The project is not purely nostalgic or optimistic.

It also contains:

- decay
- glitch
- technological leftovers
- systems that are too complicated
- industrial/cyberpunk energy
- a future built on aging infrastructure
- humor and play in a world that feels unstable

The design response is not:

> everything is hopeless

It is closer to:

> things are broken, so build something joyful from the pieces.

---

# 21. The Human Layer

The project should avoid becoming purely robotic or machine-fetishistic.

Important human qualities:

- warmth
- humor
- play
- imperfection
- memory
- faith/spiritual resonance where appropriate
- tactility
- personality
- emotional attachment to objects
- art for the sake of delight

A beautiful technical system is not enough.

It should feel lived in.

Likely repository classes:

```text

Kitty config/project
Fastfetch config/project
Quickshell project
Neovim project
Discord/Vesktop theme project
possibly GTK/icon/theme projects
```

Before naming each one, ask:

```text
1. What is the underlying technology?
2. What role does it play?
3. Which Meta Apollo Logos sub-language fits it?
4. Does the name still make sense to someone who finds the repo directly?
5. Is the GitHub slug easy to type?
6. Is the display name fun to say?
```

# 27. Naming Rule to Preserve

> **Recognizable enough to navigate, poetic enough to belong to the world.**

The ecosystem should not choose between clarity and personality when it can have both.

---

# 28. Keep the Name Playful

Meta Apollo Logos itself emerged through wordplay.

That should remain part of the culture.

Names are allowed to:

- mutate
- shorten
- become nicknames
- gain meanings over time
- be slightly weird
- sound fun
- carry multiple interpretations

The goal is not corporate consistency.

The goal is a coherent creative universe.

---

# 29. Next Naming Steps

After the base combined repo exists:

```text
1. test Meta Apollo Logos as the README/project banner
2. test possible repo slugs
3. use Metapollo on the SwayFX component
4. name component repos only when they are ready to become separate projects
5. avoid naming empty hypothetical repos too early
6. keep a shared naming vocabulary in this document
7. let recurring words emerge naturally during development
```

Naming should follow real components, not force the components to fit names.
